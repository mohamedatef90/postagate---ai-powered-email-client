# Product Overview

**Postagate** is an AI-powered email client built as a React web application. The app provides a modern, Gmail-like interface with integrated AI assistance capabilities powered by Google's Gemini API.

## Key Features

- **Multi-account email management** with themed domains (Hogwarts and Liverpool FC)
- **AI Assistant integration** for email summarization, meeting scheduling, and general assistance
- **Advanced email organization** with categories, snoozing, starring, and filtering
- **Modular interface** supporting email, chat, copilot, drive, and settings views
- **Responsive design** with mobile-friendly sidebar navigation
- **Rich composer** with reply, forward, and draft functionality
- **Search and filtering** with advanced filter options

## Target Use Case

The application serves as a demonstration/prototype of an AI-enhanced email experience, showcasing how modern AI capabilities can be integrated into traditional email workflows to improve productivity and user experience.
